Our Story

Built From Real-World Hazmat Experience

Founded on day-to-day experience in hazardous waste operations, not corporate theory.

Since 2012, our team has worked inside the hazardous waste industry supporting generators, transportation providers, and disposal facilities throughout Northern California. That experience showed a simple reality: most facilities don't need more complexity - they need a responsive point of contact that understands regulations, schedules, and budget constraints at the same time.

America Hazardous Waste Solutions was formed to close that gap. We coordinate compliant transportation and disposal options for drums, totes, bulk liquids, and solids, while keeping communication and documentation straightforward.

Today, we support manufacturers, laboratories, utilities, construction firms, public agencies, and healthcare facilities across Solano, Napa, Sonoma, Contra Costa, Yolo, and Sacramento Counties.

How We Approach Every Project

  • Prioritize safety and regulatory alignment in every movement
  • Coordinate around your production, outage, or shutdown windows
  • Give clear expectations on timelines and logistics before arrival
  • Keep communication direct - no vague status updates
  • Support your recordkeeping with accurate manifests and documentation

The objective: waste off-site on time, with the right paperwork, and no surprises.
