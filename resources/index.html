Planning Checklist

If you have these pieces outlined, you're already ahead of most facilities.

Before You Schedule a Pickup

  • Estimate quantities (drums, totes, bulk, or small containers)
  • Identify major waste types (solvents, acids, caustics, oils, lab chemicals)
  • Confirm where waste is staged and how trucks can access it
  • Note time restrictions (shift changes, school hours, patient hours)
  • Gather any SDS or previous profiles/manifests if available

You don't need every detail perfect to reach out. Approximations are fine.

Documentation That Makes Life Easier

  • Keep copies of past manifests and profiles in one place
  • Label containers clearly and consistently before pickup
  • Note any special handling concerns (reactives, cylinders, unknowns)
  • Assign one internal point of contact for waste movements
  • After each shipment, file manifests where inspectors expect them

Good documentation is less about perfection and more about consistency.
