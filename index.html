From Fairfield and Vacaville to Vallejo and Dixon, AHWS coordinates drum, tote, and bulk hazardous waste pickups for manufacturers, warehouses, and municipal facilities. We help Solano County generators move waste off-site safely while staying compliant with DOT and California regulations.
